07 / AVAILABLE RETAIL SPACE

Which properties have space available for lease?

Q1 2026 available retail suites grouped by individual size: Small (≤10K SF), Medium (>10K–30K SF), and Large (>30K SF). Pad Sites appear in a separate, overlapping column; Mixed Sizes are subdivided by size combination.

—PROPERTIES WITH AVAILABILITY
⌕
—

Source: Ocean State Job Lot Retail Real Estate Availability, Q1 2026. Vacant rate = listed available SF ÷ total GLA. “≤” indicates a potential maximum based on ranges/“up to”; “≥” excludes pad sites with unspecified SF; “~” marks an upper-range calculation that also excludes an unsized pad site. Pad-site-only listings have no calculable SF-based rate. Properties offering a pad site and a retail suite appear in both relevant columns; column counts therefore may exceed the unique-property total. Mixed Sizes classify retail area only and are subdivided into Small + Medium, Small + Large, Medium + Large, or Small + Medium + Large. These are historical advertised availabilities, not confirmed current vacancies.
